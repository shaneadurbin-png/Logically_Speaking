# Ledger

Every decision, with what it was measured against. **L** is settled; **Q** is open.

## Settled

- **L-01 · Rate key is Sage's.** A rate is keyed by rate table code (`#225121`), certified class (`#LAB-J`) and pay ID as HH2 writes it (`UNION REG`). Replaced the first draft's job | trade | class | pay-type-name key after the Sage rate table exports (SBN, BWI, AUS, PHL, DFW) showed that is how Sage prices them and that tables are shared by jobs. The Sage export reader skips the catch-all `*` rows and chains escalation dates into ranges.
- **L-02 · Class from the badge prefix, overridable; the prefixes are a setting.** Settings › Employees › Prefix defaults says what each prefix means (seeded FB2, FB5, TTR → `#LAB-J`; FB7, FB8 → `#CARP-J`; the longest listed prefix the number starts with wins). FB1 and prefixes not listed → held *no class* until the prefix is listed or the person's class is set. Foremen, general foremen, apprentices and superintendents are set per employee, and a class set on the person beats the prefix.
- **L-03 · HH2 rows are never deduplicated.** The real export carries exact duplicate rows that are real split entries; the fixture carries three. Recount on finalize checks rows and hours, not distinct rows.
- **L-04 · HH2 has 12 columns; 14 with ChildJob accepted.** The real export has 12 (no ChildJob/ChildJobName); the first spec said 14. Both read; anything else is refused naming the first wrong column. The real pay IDs carry no `#`.
- **L-05 · PTO is held, not priced and not dropped.** Vacation, Holiday, Sick Time, Flex Paid Time Off, Birthday Time Off, Floating Hol: hours kept for the audit, no cost. Overridable per pay type (rated / held / excluded).
- **L-06 · Rental month = run-rate at the latest snapshot on or before month end.** Not prorated; off-rent items are listed with the day they went off, derived from the first later report that lacks them. Two reports from one vendor as of the same day: the one recorded last stands (SQL and JS agree; `finalize_upload` stamps `clock_timestamp()` so the order is real even inside one transaction).
- **L-07 · A 4-week rate × quantity is the month.** Sunbelt's export carries a 4-week rate, no monthly figure; it is treated as the month, as the client is billed. Day- and week-only lines are shown and not totalled.
- **L-08 · Tax and markup belong to the job, taxable to the vendor.** Tax is the site's sales tax (7% at CDR), markup Liberty's (10% on rent + tax), both per job in Settings; a vendor can be untaxed. Lines under no job carry neither. Liberty-owned equipment (flag per vendor or per line) is rent only.
- **L-09 · Vendor identified from content, never from the file name.** Each layout has a header fingerprint and an identifying column (Sunbelt's Customer Name on the account export and its Cat-Class codes on the all-jobs export, United Rentals' Equipment Source, Herc's Vendor, EquipmentShare's export shape). Confirmed against a real export: United Rentals, Sunbelt, Herc, EquipmentShare. Awaiting one: Mission Critical Warehouse (refuses until then).
- **L-10 · As-of by layout.** United Rentals and EquipmentShare: the file name. Herc: the Report Date column (the name's date is the schedule's, not the data's). Sunbelt's account export: asked on the card. Sunbelt's all-jobs export and EquipmentShare's On Rent Report: the start date + days on rent, the same on every line (lines that disagree make the card ask). The page's own CSV: the name.
- **L-11 · Rental identity carries a sequence.** vendor | equipment | contract | vendor job label | seq, because United Rentals lists non-serialised bulk items as repeated identical lines; repeats are numbered in order. Bulk items with no unit number are kept by their category-class code.
- **L-12 · The Purchase Pro export is a snapshot of every PO.** The latest export (by its as-of, then by when it was recorded) stands for all POs; earlier exports stay on file. Material and Rental POs count at Sage's committed total; quotes and cancelled POs are excluded; a PO with no amount or on an unknown job waits for a decision; "NONBILLABLE" in the description is the Non-Billables bucket. Rental POs are shown apart from the on-rent reports.
- **L-13 · Jobs are company-wide, with campus and region.** The Projects register (Job #, Project Name, Campus, Region) adds jobs; the Portfolio groups by campus. The page is not only CDR.
- **L-14 · Buckets are GRforecast's.** LABOR, MATERIALS, EQUIPMENT, SUBCONTRACTORS, OTHER, NON_BILLABLE, same names and colours, so `v_month_buckets` is the forecast's import shape.
- **L-15 · Views scope themselves.** Every public view runs with its owner's rights and filters by membership (aggregates) or editor rights (lines), because a security-invoker view nested in an owner view emptied every aggregate for a viewer. RLS still guards the tables.
- **L-16 · Refusals are atomic.** `finalize_upload` raises and rolls back on any recount mismatch; the upload stays pending, nothing half-recorded.
- **L-17 · Actuals are never edited.** Labor lines, on-rent lines and purchase docs have forbid triggers; superseding keeps the old upload with the reason and who gave it.
- **L-18 · Money in integer cents, rounded half away from zero**, matching Postgres `round()`; the labor and rental parity tests hold the JS models to the SQL views to the cent.
- **L-19 · Readers run in the page, not on a server.** Same code in the browser and in Node, same tests; the database recounts what the page sends.
- **L-20 · Public repo, no real data.** Fixtures are generated; real exports, rate sheets, the register and email live only in the session's scratch space.

- **L-21 · A purchase decision is about the PO, not the export.** Every Purchase Pro export recreates every PO, so a decision keyed to one export's row would vanish a week later. `app.purchase_docs_resolved` takes the latest decision on the document or on the same PO number (same source and kind) from any export; the demo adapter does the same. A PO with no committed amount keeps waiting even when assigned a job: nothing is counted at zero.
- **L-22 · CI runs what a contributor runs.** `npm test`, `tests/db/run.sh` against a `postgres:16` service (the script's `PGURL` mode, validated over TCP locally before it was pushed) and the browser smoke straight from the files, no server.

- **L-23 · The Job Cost To Date is read whole and the latest per job stands.** Sage's export (27 columns, one sheet, values padded with spaces, two date spellings) is cumulative, so the latest recorded export per job replaces the one before; the as-of is the file name's date, else the latest Date Stamp. A payroll row's Description is the employee's name: it goes to Employees and the line keeps the number only; lines are editors'. Checked on five real exports (DC4, DC5, DC7, DC7 TFO, Site): every row read, totals to the cent.
- **L-24 · A recurring charge is one exact amount, monthly, on equipment.** Vendor invoices (AP cost) and Liberty's internal charges (IV cost) in Cat EQU, grouped by job, vendor, normalised line and amount; two or more months with at most one missing between first and last; a credit nets against the earliest charge on the same invoice (a "(Rev)" prefix is the same line); units are the lines in the latest month, and a month with more than twice that many is not one charge a month. Measured on DC4: 255 candidates, 6 off the feeds (Mobile Air's contract minimum, Altorfer's generator, the Polaris), the rest United Rentals and Sunbelt cycle invoices. Services and lodging (SUB, OTH) and varying amounts are left out on purpose; a near-equal-amount pass is not done. The SQL (`app.recurring_candidates`) and the JS (`app/recurring_model.js`) are held to each other on the fixture.
- **L-25 · A confirmed charge counts as rent with the job's markup.** From its first month to its last (open = still running), markup under the job's rule, tax only when the amount does not already carry it (an invoice does), Liberty-owned rent only. A charge from a vendor on a feed is shown for comparison and never counted: the on-rent report is the source for those. Setting one aside is kept, with the reason, so it does not come back.
- **L-26 · Rental POs are apart; purchases are material POs.** The weekly cost workbook partitioned the PO universe the same way (Committed = Material, OffFeedRental = Rental); the rental itself is on a feed or a recurring charge, so a rental PO in purchases would count it twice. A new job's rental tax starts at its campus's rate from that workbook (CDR 7%, PHL 6%, BWI 6%, SBN 0%, DFW2 and AUS 8.25%), edited in Settings.

- **L-27 · Site services are read off the rental lines by their words.** The vocabulary was measured on the real United Rentals All Jobs export (4,594 lines) and five ledgers: STANDARD / HIGH RISE / ELEVATOR FIT / HANDICAP / ENHANCED PORTABLE RESTROOM, restroom trailers and STATIC RESTROOM with n STATION, SINK, WASTE HOLDING TANK; MODULAR BLDG FAST FRONT / MIDDLE / REAR (and Sage's MODULAR FAST, FRNT QTS PLEX, n-PLEX spellings), MODULAR BLDG 24X60 W/2-RR, OFFICE TRAILER 12X60 W/RR, GLO/OFFICE CONTAINER, CONTAINER 8X40; SERVICE - RESTROOM nX WEEKLY is a service, OFFICE TRAILER STEPS and CONTAINMENT TRAY are accessories, TRAILER EQUIP, TRAILER WATER TANK, LIGHT TOWER ... TRAILER and a ROLLOFF TANK are not ours. The sleeves under one contract make an x-plex: complexes = the fronts that have a rear, sections = all sections over the complexes; a mismatch is flagged. `app.classify_rental` and `app/site_services.js` are held to each other on 56 words. A Settings override for a vendor's odd word is not built yet (Q-08).
- **L-28 · The dumpster month: the log over the ledger.** A hauler that invoices one pull at a time (Sourgum: $685 an invoice, 504 of 623 lines on DC5) is counted off the ledger, a pull a line, a credit taking one back; a hauler that bills a lump (Waste Management, twice a month, split across jobs) shows spend and no count. Liberty's own log (pull date, hauler, size, pulls, ticket; cost and tonnage optional) wins for any hauler and month it has entries for, and the page names the source. A hauler's lines are never a recurring rental (L-24 excludes them). Decided with Shane on 2026-10-02.
- **L-29 · A line whose quantity the export does not say is shown, not counted.** Sunbelt's all-jobs export has no Quantity column: a line with a serial number is one unit, a line without (cable, deck panels, mats) is one contract line that may be many units (checked against the account export of the same day: every serialised line was one unit, bulk lines ran 4 to 90). Such a line shows its 4-week rate, is counted among "no monthly figure", and is marked on the statement; the account export carries quantities and reads as before. The old workbook counted each as one.
- **L-30 · A PO with no job is never a refusal.** Purchase Pro lets a PO carry no Job (quotes often do, and a live order can be cut before the job is coded). A quote or a cancelled order with no job is left out, as it would be anyway; a live order with no job records with no job and waits for a decision, the same way an order on a job Settings does not know waits. The card counts them. (Found on the full Tbl_PO1 export of 2026-10-02: 9,617 POs, refused for one quote.)
- **L-31 · OrderID is the order; a PO number can be on two orders.** Tbl_PO has no primary key, and the export of 2026-10-02 carries 32 numbers that sit on two or more different orders ("26.01" on five). The same OrderID also sits on a quote and the order it became (7 do), and one export carried one order twice with different details. Nothing refuses: every row is kept, every order whose number is on more than one row is marked, and all of them wait for a decision until Purchase Pro gives each its own number. A decision is about one order, so it carries across exports by number and OrderID, never by number alone.
- **L-32 · A month with no on-rent report by its last day takes the first report after it, within a week.** The weekly package is pulled a day or two into the next month, and that report is the best word on what was on rent at month end; a report by the month's end still wins, and two as of the same day keep the "recorded last" rule. The tile says "first report after the month". The same rule in `rentals_model.snapshotForMonth` and in `v_rental_month` / `rental_month_lines` (0015). Nine days after is too late: the month shows nothing rather than a guess.
